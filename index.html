What it does

Automatic syllabus analysis

Upload your syllabus as a PDF and the grading structure is read out for you — assessments, weights and dates.

Academic risk detection

Every course is scored from your real grades and weights, so you know which ones actually need attention.

Personalized study planning

A weekly schedule built around the hours you actually have and the deadlines closest to you.

Adaptive recovery

Enter a new grade and the whole plan is reassessed and rewritten — not generated once and forgotten.

Study recommendations

Specific topics to work on next, drawn from the assessments you have already sat.
